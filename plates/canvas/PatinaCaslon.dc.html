Best Book Design from all over the World 2026

Announced 12 March 2026 and awarded at the Leipzig Book Fair on 20 March. Around 570 books from 33 nations were reviewed by Sunandini Banerjee, Artur Frankowski, Fraser Muggeridge, Isabel Seiffert and Raül Vicent.1 Only books that have already won a national competition are eligible, which makes this the single highest-signal book design award. The measure is set once, as --md-measure, and TDC medals are counted in four three disciplines. Press ⌘K to search; the Goldene Letter is the top prize.
